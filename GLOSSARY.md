# Meeting Slot Booking

This context coordinates one-off meeting bookings by letting organizers publish available times and clients book them without creating an account.

## Language

**Organizer**:
A person who offers meeting slots and manages the resulting bookings.

**Client**:
A person who books an offered meeting slot and is identified for the booking by the email address they provide.

**Slot**:
A specific dated time interval that an organizer offers for one meeting.

**Hold**:
A temporary exclusive claim on an available slot created when a client selects it; it lasts up to one minute and becomes a booking after confirmation or expires.

**Booking**:
A confirmed reservation of one organizer's slot for one client.

**Organizer management key**:
A private secret that proves its holder can manage one organizer's slots and bookings.
